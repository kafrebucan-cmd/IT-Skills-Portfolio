About This Portfolio

Hello! I am Keanna Alexandria Rebucan. This portfolio presents the three activities I completed during the Prelim period of Elective 1 – IT Skills. Each activity allowed me to explore different ways of communicating ideas through visual design.

Prelim Activities

ACTIVITY 01

Presentation Design Principles

A promotional design for a Free Coffee event. I applied visual hierarchy, contrast, balance, proximity, and consistency to organize the information and make the main message stand out.

ACTIVITY 02

Color Palette & Typography

A personal branding concept featuring a logo, tagline, color palette, and typography set. The design combines dark green, black, neutral tones, and different typefaces to create a professional and sophisticated identity.

ACTIVITY 03

Social Media Infographic

An infographic focused on depression and the importance of support groups. The main message, “YOU ARE NOT ALONE,” was supported through organized sections, contrasting colors, illustrations, and clear typography.

Reflection

These activities taught me that effective design is not only about making something visually attractive. It is also about organizing information, choosing appropriate visual elements, and communicating a message clearly to the audience.
